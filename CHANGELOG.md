# Gamepad Enhancer

## Unreleased
- New: hide the controller's action bars out of combat, except the two shoulder icons. They show faded (opacity you choose) while your target is in range of one of their spells, and fully in a fight. Options > AddOns > Gamepad Enhancer, or /gpen.
- New: fade the two shoulder icons out of combat, with their own opacity.
- New: fade time for the bars and shoulder icons, 0 to 10 seconds (2 s by default).
- New: "In range" opacity, so the bars show more once your target is in range of one of their spells. "With a target" sets how they look before that.
- New: "Show on triggers": holding a trigger or bumper out of combat shows the bars, even with no target.
- New: "Trigger opacity", how visible the bars are while you hold a trigger or bumper (40% by default).
- New: "Trigger fade", how fast the bars show or hide on a trigger or bumper (0.1 s by default).
- On by default: bars hidden out of combat (40% with a target, 70% in range), shoulder icons at 60%, "Show on triggers".
